Common Questions

Everything you need to know about GBT AI Stack pricing and billing.

How does instant settlement work?

Pay with USDC on Polygon (~2s) or USDT on TRC-20 (~3s). Payment is confirmed on-chain, and your AI tool deploys immediately. No waiting for bank processing or manual approval.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Yes. We accept Visa and Mastercard via Coinflow/CryptAPI. Fiat payments take T+2 business days to settle to your account, but AI tools deploy instantly on payment confirmation.

What does the revenue share mean?

For developers listing open-source projects on GBT, you earn 80% of the hourly rental fee (up to 85% on Enterprise). Our SaaS subscription plans ($29-$299/mo) are for teams deploying AI tools. If you're a developer listing projects, there are no monthly fees — just the 80/20 split. See onboarding →

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. Cancel anytime — your tools stay live until the end of the billing period. No cancellation fees.

Do you support Shopify integration?

Yes. We're an official Shopify Partner. Embed our AI tools directly in your Shopify store with our Buy Button or headless Storefront API integration.

Where are you based and what currencies?

London, United Kingdom. We serve customers globally. All prices are in USD. Crypto payments have no geographic restrictions — anyone can pay from anywhere.
